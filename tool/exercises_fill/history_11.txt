Q: In which year did Germany suffer hyperinflation so severe that money became almost worthless?
+ 1923
- 1914
- 1933
- 1945
E: In 1923, after the French occupation of the Ruhr and huge money printing by the Weimar government, prices rose so fast that people needed wheelbarrows of banknotes to buy bread. The Dawes Plan of 1924 helped stabilise the economy.

Q: When was Adolf Hitler appointed Chancellor of Germany?
+ January 1933
- November 1918
- October 1929
- September 1939
E: President Hindenburg appointed Hitler Chancellor on 30 January 1933. Within months the Nazis used the Reichstag fire and the Enabling Act to make Hitler a dictator. 1918 was the end of WWI, 1929 the Wall Street Crash, and 1939 the start of WWII.

Q: What were Stalin's Five-Year Plans in the Soviet Union (from 1928)?
+ state plans for rapid industrialisation, with collectivisation of agriculture
- plans to restore the Tsar
- free-market reforms that ended state control
- treaties of friendship with Britain and France
E: Stalin's Five-Year Plans set high targets for heavy industry (coal, steel, electricity). Peasant farms were forced into collective farms. Industry grew fast, but millions suffered and died, especially in the famine of 1932–1933.

Q: Which event started the Second World War in Europe on 1 September 1939?
+ Germany's invasion of Poland
- Japan's attack on Pearl Harbor
- Italy's invasion of Ethiopia
- the German invasion of the Soviet Union
E: Germany invaded Poland on 1 September 1939; Britain and France declared war on Germany two days later. Pearl Harbor was in December 1941, Italy invaded Ethiopia in 1935, and Germany invaded the USSR in June 1941.

Q: The Battle of Keren (February–March 1941) was important for Eritrean history because:
+ British forces defeated the Italians, ending Italian colonial rule in Eritrea
- Italy conquered Eritrea for the first time
- Eritrea became independent immediately
- Germany captured Massawa
E: At Keren, British and Commonwealth forces broke the Italian defences after weeks of hard fighting. Asmara and Massawa fell soon after, and Eritrea came under British Military Administration (1941–1952).

Q: The German invasion of the Soviet Union in June 1941 was code-named:
+ Operation Barbarossa
- Operation Overlord
- Operation Sea Lion
- the Blitz
E: Operation Barbarossa began on 22 June 1941. Overlord was the D-Day invasion of Normandy (1944), Sea Lion was the planned (never carried out) invasion of Britain, and the Blitz was the bombing of British cities.

Q: When did Germany surrender unconditionally, ending the war in Europe?
+ May 1945
- June 1944
- August 1945
- November 1918
E: After Hitler's suicide in Berlin, Germany surrendered on 7–8 May 1945 (VE Day). Japan surrendered in August 1945 after the atomic bombs. June 1944 was D-Day, and November 1918 ended WWI.

Q: Which countries were the three main Axis powers in the Second World War?
+ Germany, Italy and Japan
- Britain, France and the USA
- the USSR, China and Britain
- Germany, Austria-Hungary and the Ottoman Empire
E: Germany, Italy and Japan formed the Axis. The main Allies were Britain, the USSR, the USA and China. Germany, Austria-Hungary and the Ottoman Empire were the Central Powers of the First World War.
