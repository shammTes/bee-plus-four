C: Because of the Earth's rotation, the local time of a place 30° east of Greenwich is:
O: *2 hours ahead of Greenwich | 2 hours behind Greenwich | 30 minutes ahead of Greenwich | the same as Greenwich
W: The Earth turns 360° in 24 hours, i.e. 15° per hour. | 30° ÷ 15° per hour = 2 hours. | Places to the east see the Sun earlier, so they are ahead. | Tip: East – gain time (add); west – lose time (subtract).

C: Air temperature normally decreases with altitude at an average rate of about:
O: *6.5 °C per 1000 m | 1 °C per 1000 m | 6.5 °C per 100 m | 15 °C per 1000 m
W: Air is heated mainly from the ground, and higher air is thinner and holds less heat. | The normal (environmental) lapse rate is about 6.5 °C per 1000 m. | Answer: 6.5 °C per 1000 m. | Tip: Use it to compare Massawa and Asmara: about 2.35 km × 6.5 ≈ 15 °C difference.
