C: In the female reproductive system, fertilization normally takes place in the:
O: *oviduct (fallopian tube) | uterus | ovary | vagina
W: The ovary releases an egg into the oviduct. | Sperm swim up through the uterus into the oviduct, where they meet the egg. | The zygote then moves to the uterus and implants in its lining. | Tip: Implantation happens in the uterus, fertilization in the oviduct – do not confuse them.

C: Which contraceptive method also protects against sexually transmitted infections such as HIV?
O: *Condom | Contraceptive pill | Intrauterine device (IUD) | Vasectomy
W: Only barrier methods stop body fluids from passing between partners. | The pill, IUD and vasectomy prevent pregnancy but give no protection against infections. | Answer: the condom. | Tip: Abstinence is the only 100 % effective way to prevent both pregnancy and STIs.

C: In a dicot stem, the vascular bundles are arranged:
O: *in a ring | scattered throughout the stem | only at the centre | only in the epidermis
W: Dicot stems have vascular bundles arranged in a ring, with cambium between xylem and phloem. | Monocot stems have scattered vascular bundles and no cambium. | Answer: in a ring. | Tip: The cambium in the ring lets dicot stems grow thicker (secondary growth).
