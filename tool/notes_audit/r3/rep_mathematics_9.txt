@lesson math9-u1-l1-2
C: Which pair of sets is equivalent but NOT equal?
O: *A = {1, 2, 3} and B = {a, b, c} | A = {1, 2, 3} and B = {3, 2, 1} | A = {a, b} and B = {a, b, c} | A = {} and B = {0}
W: Equal sets have exactly the same elements; equivalent sets only have the same number of elements. | {1, 2, 3} and {a, b, c} both have 3 elements but different elements → equivalent, not equal. | {1, 2, 3} and {3, 2, 1} are equal (order does not matter). | Tip: Every pair of equal sets is also equivalent, but not every equivalent pair is equal.

@lesson math9-u3-l3-3
C: The slope of the line through the points (1, 2) and (3, 8) is:
O: *3 | 1/3 | 6 | −3
W: Formula: m = (y₂ − y₁) ÷ (x₂ − x₁). | Substitute: m = (8 − 2) ÷ (3 − 1) = 6 ÷ 2. | Answer: m = 3. | Tip: A positive slope rises from left to right; a horizontal line has slope 0.

@lesson math9-u6-l6-2
C: A ladder 10 m long leans against a wall with its foot 6 m from the wall. How high up the wall does it reach?
O: *8 m | 4 m | 16 m | 11.7 m
W: The ladder is the hypotenuse: c² = a² + b². | 10² = 6² + h² → h² = 100 − 36 = 64. | h = √64 = 8 m. | Tip: Look out for Pythagorean triples such as 3-4-5, 6-8-10 and 5-12-13.

@lesson math9-u7-l7-3
C: A trader buys a radio for 400 Nakfa and sells it for 500 Nakfa. The profit percentage is:
O: *25% | 20% | 100% | 80%
W: Profit = selling price − cost price = 500 − 400 = 100 Nakfa. | Profit % = (profit ÷ cost price) × 100 % = (100 ÷ 400) × 100 %. | Answer: 25 %. | Tip: Profit or loss percentage is always calculated on the cost price unless the question says otherwise.

@lesson math9-u7-l7-4
C: The simple interest on 2000 Nakfa at 5% per year for 3 years is:
O: *300 Nakfa | 30 Nakfa | 3000 Nakfa | 2300 Nakfa
W: Formula: I = P × R × T ÷ 100. | Substitute: I = 2000 × 5 × 3 ÷ 100. | Answer: I = 300 Nakfa. | Tip: The total amount A = P + I = 2300 Nakfa – do not confuse the interest with the amount.

C: At what rate of simple interest will 1500 Nakfa earn 180 Nakfa interest in 2 years?
O: *6% | 12% | 3% | 9%
W: Formula: R = (I × 100) ÷ (P × T). | Substitute: R = (180 × 100) ÷ (1500 × 2) = 18 000 ÷ 3000. | Answer: R = 6 % per year. | Tip: Rearrange I = PRT/100 before substituting to avoid arithmetic slips.

WK: Finding the time for simple interest
P: How long will it take 800 Nakfa to amount to 1040 Nakfa at 10% simple interest per year?
ST: Interest earned: I = A − P = 1040 − 800 = 240 Nakfa. | Formula: T = (I × 100) ÷ (P × R). | Substitute: T = (240 × 100) ÷ (800 × 10) = 24 000 ÷ 8000. | T = 3.
A: 3 years.
