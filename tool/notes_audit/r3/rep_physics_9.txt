C: A 0.5 kg ball moving at 4 m/s has a momentum of:
O: *2 kg·m/s | 8 kg·m/s | 0.125 kg·m/s | 4.5 kg·m/s
W: Formula: p = m × v. | Substitute: p = 0.5 kg × 4 m/s. | Answer: p = 2 kg·m/s. | Tip: Momentum is a vector – it has the direction of the velocity.

C: A force of 20 N acts on a body for 0.3 s. The impulse given to the body is:
O: *6 N·s | 66.7 N·s | 20.3 N·s | 0.015 N·s
W: Formula: impulse J = F × t. | Substitute: J = 20 N × 0.3 s. | Answer: J = 6 N·s, which equals the change in momentum. | Tip: 1 N·s = 1 kg·m/s, so impulse and momentum change have the same unit.

WK: Change in momentum and average force
P: A 0.2 kg ball hits a wall at 10 m/s and bounces straight back at 8 m/s. The contact time is 0.05 s. Find the change in momentum and the average force on the ball.
ST: Take the direction towards the wall as positive: u = +10 m/s, v = −8 m/s. | Change in momentum: Δp = m(v − u) = 0.2 × (−8 − 10) = −3.6 kg·m/s. | Average force: F = Δp ÷ t = −3.6 ÷ 0.05 = −72 N. | The minus sign means the force on the ball points away from the wall.
A: Δp = 3.6 kg·m/s away from the wall; average force = 72 N away from the wall.

C: A box is pushed 5 m along the floor by a horizontal force of 30 N. The work done by the force is:
O: *150 J | 6 J | 35 J | 0.17 J
W: Formula: W = F × d (force along the displacement). | Substitute: W = 30 N × 5 m. | Answer: W = 150 J. | Tip: If the force is perpendicular to the motion (e.g. carrying a bag horizontally), the work done by that force is zero.
