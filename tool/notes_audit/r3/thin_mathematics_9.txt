@lesson math9-u3-l3-3
TITLE: Slope of a linear function
S: Slope m measures steepness: m = rise ÷ run = (y₂ − y₁) ÷ (x₂ − x₁). | In y = mx + c, m is the slope and c is the y-intercept. | m > 0: the line rises to the right; m < 0: it falls; m = 0: horizontal; vertical lines have undefined slope. | Parallel lines have equal slopes; perpendicular lines have slopes whose product is −1. | To find the equation through two points, find m first, then substitute one point to find c.
E: Line through (2, 1) and (4, 7): m = (7 − 1) ÷ (4 − 2) = 3. Then 1 = 3(2) + c → c = −5, so y = 3x − 5.
M: "Rise over Run" — up-down change on top, left-right change below.
T: Keep the same order of points in the numerator and denominator; mixing the order changes the sign of the slope.

@lesson math9-u6-l6-2
TITLE: Solving problems with Pythagoras' theorem
S: Draw a sketch and mark the right angle. | Identify the hypotenuse c: the longest side, opposite the right angle. | Write c² = a² + b². | To find the hypotenuse: c = √(a² + b²); to find a shorter side: a = √(c² − b²). | Give the answer with units and check that the hypotenuse is the longest side.
E: A rectangular field is 40 m by 30 m. The diagonal path is √(40² + 30²) = √(1600 + 900) = √2500 = 50 m.
M: "Hypotenuse: add the squares; short side: subtract the squares."
T: Converse: if c² = a² + b² for three sides, the triangle is right-angled – use it to test triangles such as 5, 12, 13.

@lesson math9-u7-l7-3
TITLE: Profit and loss
S: Cost price (CP) is what the trader pays; selling price (SP) is what the customer pays. | If SP > CP: profit = SP − CP; if SP < CP: loss = CP − SP. | Profit % = profit ÷ CP × 100 %; loss % = loss ÷ CP × 100 %. | To find SP for a given profit %: SP = CP × (100 + profit %) ÷ 100. | To find CP from SP: CP = SP × 100 ÷ (100 + profit %).
E: A shirt costs 250 Nakfa and the trader wants 20 % profit: SP = 250 × 120 ÷ 100 = 300 Nakfa. If it is later sold for 225 Nakfa: loss = 25 Nakfa, loss % = 25 ÷ 250 × 100 = 10 %.
M: "Profit and loss are percentages of the Cost" — always divide by CP.
T: Write CP and SP clearly at the start of every problem; most errors come from dividing by SP instead of CP.

@lesson math9-u7-l7-4
TITLE: Simple interest
S: Principal P is the money borrowed or saved; rate R is the percentage per year; time T is in years. | Simple interest I = P × R × T ÷ 100. | Amount A = P + I. | Rearranged: P = 100I ÷ (RT), R = 100I ÷ (PT), T = 100I ÷ (PR). | Convert months to years (÷ 12) before substituting.
E: 5000 Nakfa at 8 % for 9 months: T = 9/12 = 0.75 years; I = 5000 × 8 × 0.75 ÷ 100 = 300 Nakfa; A = 5300 Nakfa.
M: "I = PRT over 100" — "Pay Rent Today".
T: Read whether the question asks for the interest or the total amount – both are common and they are easy to confuse.
