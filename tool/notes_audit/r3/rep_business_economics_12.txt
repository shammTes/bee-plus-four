C: Countries gain from international trade mainly because of:
O: *specialization according to comparative advantage | equal prices in every country | having no need for money | avoiding all imports

@lesson be12-u2-l2-3
C: A machine costs 60 000 Nakfa, has a salvage value of 10 000 Nakfa and a useful life of 5 years. The annual straight-line depreciation is:
O: *10 000 Nakfa | 12 000 Nakfa | 14 000 Nakfa | 50 000 Nakfa
W: Straight-line depreciation = (cost − salvage value) ÷ useful life. | = (60 000 − 10 000) ÷ 5 = 50 000 ÷ 5. | Answer: 10 000 Nakfa per year. | Tip: Land is a plant asset that is NOT depreciated.
