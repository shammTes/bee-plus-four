C: A block of wood of mass 2 kg floats in water. The buoyant force acting on it is (g = 10 m/s²):
O: *20 N | 2 N | 0 N | 200 N
W: Law of flotation: a floating body displaces its own weight of fluid, so buoyant force = weight. | Weight = mg = 2 kg × 10 m/s² = 20 N. | Answer: buoyant force = 20 N. | Tip: A body floats when its average density is less than that of the fluid.

C: In the hydrogen atom, a line of the emission spectrum is produced when an electron:
O: *falls from a higher to a lower energy level | moves from a lower to a higher energy level | leaves the nucleus | stays in its ground state
W: Each energy level has a fixed energy. | When an electron drops to a lower level it emits a photon with energy E = hf = E_high − E_low. | Answer: falling to a lower level gives an emission line. | Tip: Absorption lines are produced by the reverse jump (low → high).
