C: According to the law of demand, when the price of a good rises (other things equal), the quantity demanded:
O: *falls | rises | stays the same | first rises then falls
W: The law of demand states an inverse relationship between price and quantity demanded. | Higher price → consumers buy less (substitution and income effects). | Answer: quantity demanded falls. | Tip: A change in price moves along the demand curve; a change in income or tastes shifts the whole curve.

C: A firm's fixed costs are 1000 Nakfa and its variable cost is 20 Nakfa per unit. The total cost of producing 50 units is:
O: *2000 Nakfa | 1020 Nakfa | 1050 Nakfa | 3000 Nakfa
W: Total cost TC = fixed cost + variable cost. | Variable cost = 20 × 50 = 1000 Nakfa. | TC = 1000 + 1000 = 2000 Nakfa. | Tip: Fixed costs (rent, salaries) do not change with output; variable costs (raw materials) do.

C: Which transaction is recorded in the cash receipts journal?
O: *Cash received from a customer on account | Purchase of goods on credit | Payment of rent in cash | Sale of goods on credit
W: The cash receipts journal records ALL cash coming into the business. | Cash from a customer settling an account is a cash receipt. | Credit sales go to the sales journal, credit purchases to the purchases journal, cash payments to the cash payments journal. | Tip: Ask "is cash coming in?" – if yes, it belongs in the cash receipts journal.
