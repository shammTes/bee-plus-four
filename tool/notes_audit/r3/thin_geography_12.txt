TITLE: Environmental issues in Eritrea
S: Identify the problem: deforestation, soil erosion, land degradation, loss of wildlife and desertification. | Causes: fuel-wood and charcoal use, clearing for farming, overgrazing, recurrent drought, the thirty-year war and rapid urban growth. | Consequences: fuel shortages (dung and crop residues burned instead of returned to the soil), falling soil fertility, silting of dams, loss of habitats and wildlife. | Solutions: afforestation and hillside closures, terracing and check dams, improved stoves and alternative energy, controlled grazing and protected areas. | Community participation, environmental education and laws make the solutions last.
E: Burning dung for cooking instead of using it as manure is a chain effect of deforestation: no trees → no fuel-wood → dung burned → less manure → lower crop yields.
M: "Cut, Erode, Dry, Lose" — deforestation, erosion, desertification, loss of wildlife: the four linked problems.
T: Structure answers as problem → cause → consequence → solution; examiners give marks for each link.
