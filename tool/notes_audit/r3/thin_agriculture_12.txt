TITLE: Animal housing and equipment
S: Purpose of housing: protect animals from weather (sun, rain, cold wind), predators and theft; make feeding, milking, disease control and record keeping easier. | Choose the site: well-drained, slightly raised ground, near water and feed, easy to reach, away from homes downwind for smell. | Good house requirements: enough space per animal, good ventilation and light, a sloping, easy-to-clean floor with drainage, a strong roof, and separate pens for young, sick or breeding animals. | Equipment: feeders and water troughs, milking buckets and stools, dipping tank or spray for ticks, crush for handling, weighing scale, and cleaning tools. | Keep it hygienic: remove dung daily, disinfect regularly, isolate sick animals, and provide clean bedding.
E: A dairy farmer near Asmara builds a cow shed on a gentle slope with a concrete floor sloping to a drain, open sides with wire for ventilation, a feed trough along the front and a separate calf pen – cows stay clean, milk stays clean and mastitis is reduced.
M: "SPACE" — Site, Protection, Air (ventilation), Cleaning (drainage), Equipment.
T: When asked to describe a good animal house, give each feature with its reason (e.g. "sloping floor – so urine and water drain away and the floor stays dry").
