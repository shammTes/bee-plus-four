C: Nuclear fusion is the process in which:
O: *light nuclei join to form a heavier nucleus | a heavy nucleus splits into lighter nuclei | an electron is emitted from the nucleus | a nucleus absorbs a gamma ray only
W: Fusion joins light nuclei (e.g. isotopes of hydrogen) into a heavier nucleus (helium). | A small amount of mass is lost and released as energy (E = mc²). | Answer: light nuclei join. | Tip: Splitting heavy nuclei is fission, the opposite process.

C: Why does nuclear fusion require extremely high temperatures?
O: *To overcome the electrostatic repulsion between positively charged nuclei | To break the chemical bonds in hydrogen molecules | To slow down the neutrons | To melt the reactor walls
W: All nuclei are positively charged and repel each other. | Only at millions of degrees do nuclei move fast enough to get close enough for the strong nuclear force to join them. | Answer: to overcome electrostatic repulsion. | Tip: This is why fusion happens naturally in the Sun's core (about 15 million °C).

WK: Balancing a fusion equation
P: Complete the fusion reaction ²₁H + ³₁H → ⁴₂He + X and identify X.
ST: Mass numbers must balance: 2 + 3 = 4 + A → A = 1. | Atomic numbers must balance: 1 + 1 = 2 + Z → Z = 0. | A particle with A = 1 and Z = 0 is a neutron, ¹₀n.
A: X = ¹₀n (a neutron).
