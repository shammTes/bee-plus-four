WK: Identifying the factors of production
P: A farmer near Keren grows vegetables on her land, hires two workers, uses a water pump and decides what to plant and where to sell. Identify the factors of production and the reward each one earns.
ST: Land – the farm plot and its water (natural resources); reward = rent. | Labour – the two hired workers (and the farmer's own physical work); reward = wages. | Capital – the water pump and tools (man-made aids to production); reward = interest. | Entrepreneurship – the farmer who organizes, decides and takes the risk; reward = profit.
A: Land (rent), labour (wages), capital (interest), entrepreneur (profit).
