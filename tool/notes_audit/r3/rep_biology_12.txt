@lesson bio12-u3-l3-6
C: Which of the following is a non-renewable natural resource?
O: *Petroleum (crude oil) | Solar energy | Forests | Fresh water
W: Non-renewable resources exist in limited amounts and form over millions of years. | Petroleum, coal, natural gas and most minerals are non-renewable. | Solar energy, forests and water can be replaced if managed well. | Tip: Forests are renewable but exhaustible – overuse can still destroy them.

C: Which practice best conserves natural resources?
O: *Recycling materials and using renewable energy | Burning more fuel-wood | Clearing forests for farmland | Dumping waste into rivers
W: Conservation means protecting resources and using them wisely. | Recycling saves raw materials; renewable energy (solar, wind) saves fossil fuels and reduces pollution. | The other options destroy or pollute resources. | Tip: Remember the 3 Rs: Reduce, Reuse, Recycle.

WK: Renewable vs non-renewable resources
P: Classify these resources as renewable or non-renewable and give a reason: coal, wind, fish, iron ore.
ST: Renewable resources are replaced naturally within a human lifetime if used carefully. | Wind is renewable – it is produced continuously by the Sun heating the air. Fish are renewable – they reproduce, as long as overfishing is avoided. | Coal is non-renewable – it formed over millions of years and cannot be replaced. | Iron ore is non-renewable – the mineral deposit is finite (although iron can be recycled).
A: Renewable: wind, fish. Non-renewable: coal, iron ore.
