TITLE: Cost of production
S: Fixed costs (FC) do not change with output in the short run: rent, salaries, insurance, interest. | Variable costs (VC) change with output: raw materials, fuel, wages of casual labour. | Total cost TC = FC + VC; average cost AC = TC ÷ Q; average fixed cost AFC = FC ÷ Q (falls as output rises). | Marginal cost MC = change in TC ÷ change in Q – the cost of producing one more unit. | Profit = total revenue − total cost; a firm maximizes profit where MC = MR (marginal revenue).
E: FC = 600 Nakfa, VC = 15 Nakfa per unit. At Q = 40: TC = 600 + 600 = 1200 Nakfa, AC = 1200 ÷ 40 = 30 Nakfa. At Q = 41: TC = 1215, so MC = 15 Nakfa.
M: "Fixed stays, Variable varies" — and "Average = divide by Q, Marginal = one more unit".
T: Set out cost questions as a table (Q, FC, VC, TC, AC, MC) – it shows your method and avoids arithmetic slips.
