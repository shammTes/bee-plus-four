TITLE: Energy flow in photosynthesis and respiration
S: Photosynthesis traps light energy using chlorophyll in chloroplasts: 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ (light energy → chemical energy in glucose). | Light-dependent stage: light splits water, releasing O₂ and making ATP and NADPH. | Light-independent stage (Calvin cycle): CO₂ is fixed into glucose using the ATP and NADPH. | Respiration in the mitochondria releases the energy in glucose: C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + energy (ATP). | The two processes form a cycle: the products of one are the raw materials of the other; energy flows one way (Sun → glucose → ATP → heat) while matter is recycled.
E: A plant in sunlight takes in CO₂ and gives out O₂ because photosynthesis is faster than respiration; at night it only respires, so it takes in O₂ and gives out CO₂.
M: "Photosynthesis Packs energy, Respiration Releases it" — P-P, R-R.
T: Remember the exact reverse equations. In a comparison table include: site (chloroplast/mitochondrion), raw materials, products, energy (stored/released) and time (light only/all the time).
