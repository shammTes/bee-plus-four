chem11-u1-q01|Raising the temperature gives particles more kinetic energy, so they collide more often and, more importantly, a larger fraction of collisions have energy equal to or greater than the activation energy, so the rate increases.
chem11-u1-q02|A catalyst provides an alternative reaction pathway with a lower activation energy, so more collisions succeed; it is not used up and does not change ΔH.
chem11-u1-q03|Charles's law (V ∝ T) only works with absolute temperature: in kelvin, 0 K means zero volume in theory, whereas 0 °C is not a true zero. Convert with T(K) = t(°C) + 273.
chem11-u2-q02|Zinc is more reactive than iron, so it corrodes first and gives electrons to the iron (sacrificial protection); even if the coating is scratched the iron does not rust.
