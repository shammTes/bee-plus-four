be11-u1-q01|A perfectly competitive firm is one of very many sellers of an identical product, so it cannot affect the market price: it is a price taker and faces a horizontal (perfectly elastic) demand curve.
be11-u1-q02|A drought reduces supply, shifting the supply curve to the left; at the old price there is a shortage, so the equilibrium price rises and the quantity traded falls.
be11-u1-q03||PED| < 1 means quantity demanded changes by a smaller percentage than price: a 10% price rise reduces quantity by only 2%, so demand is inelastic.
be11-u1-q04|The asset computer increases (debit Computer account) and the asset cash decreases (credit Cash account) by the same amount.
be11-u1-q05|Cost of goods sold = opening stock + purchases − closing stock: the goods available for sale minus those still unsold.
