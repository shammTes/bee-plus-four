C: A plant cell placed in pure water becomes firm (turgid). Which process causes this?
O: *Osmosis | Active transport | Diffusion of salt | Excretion
W: Pure water has a higher water potential than the cell sap. | Water moves into the cell through the partially permeable membrane by osmosis. | The cell swells against its wall and becomes turgid.

@lesson bio9-u1-l1-4
C: Bacteria are prokaryotes. This means they ____.
O: *have no true nucleus surrounded by a membrane | have a large central vacuole | are always multicellular | contain chloroplasts
W: Prokaryotic cells have their DNA free in the cytoplasm as a single loop. | They lack a nuclear membrane and membrane-bound organelles. | Bacteria are single-celled and most lack chloroplasts.

@lesson bio9-u2-l2-2
C: A dichotomous key identifies organisms by ____.
O: *a series of paired choices between contrasting features | measuring their mass | counting their cells | their scientific names only
W: "Dichotomous" means dividing into two. | At each step you choose one of two contrasting statements. | Following the choices leads to the organism's name.
