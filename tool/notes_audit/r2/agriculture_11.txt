# Agriculture 11 round 2

@lesson agri11-u2-l2-3
C: Biodiversity is best defined as ____.
O: *the variety of living organisms – genes, species and ecosystems – in an area | the number of farms in a region | the amount of rainfall a crop needs | the total area of cultivated land
W: Biodiversity covers three levels: genetic diversity, species diversity and ecosystem diversity. | It includes wild and domesticated plants, animals and micro-organisms. | Farm numbers, rainfall and land area are not measures of biodiversity.

@lesson agri11-u2-l2-5
C: Agro-ecological zones are areas grouped mainly by similar ____.
O: *climate (rainfall, temperature), altitude and soils, which decide what can be grown | population size | language | road networks
W: An agro-ecological zone has similar rainfall, temperature, altitude and soil conditions. | These factors control which crops and livestock suit the area. | Population, language and roads do not define such zones.
