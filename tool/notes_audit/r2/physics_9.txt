# Physics 9 round 2

@lesson phys9-u1-l1-2
C: How many significant figures are in 0.00450?
O: *3 | 2 | 5 | 6
W: Leading zeros only locate the decimal point and are not significant. | The digits 4 and 5 are significant. | The trailing zero after the decimal point is also significant: 3 significant figures.

@lesson phys9-u1-l1-3
C: Which of the following is a vector quantity?
O: *Displacement | Mass | Time | Temperature
W: A vector has both magnitude and direction. | Displacement tells how far and in which direction. | Mass, time and temperature have magnitude only (scalars).

@lesson phys9-u2-l2-2
C: On a velocity–time graph, the area under the graph gives the ____.
O: *displacement | acceleration | speed at the end | force
W: Area = velocity × time. | Velocity × time = displacement. | The slope of a v–t graph gives acceleration.

@lesson phys9-u3-l3-2
C: Which is a way to reduce friction between moving parts of a machine?
O: *Lubricating with oil | Making the surfaces rougher | Pressing the surfaces together harder | Removing ball bearings
W: Friction depends on surface roughness and how hard the surfaces press together. | Oil forms a thin layer that keeps the surfaces apart. | Roughening, extra pressure and removing bearings increase friction.

@lesson phys9-u3-l3-3
C: A 2 kg ball moves at 3 m/s. What is its momentum?
O: *6 kg·m/s | 1.5 kg·m/s | 5 kg·m/s | 9 kg·m/s
W: Momentum $p = mv$. | $p = 2 \times 3$. | $= 6$ kg·m/s.

@lesson phys9-u4-l4-2
C: What is the kinetic energy of a 4 kg object moving at 5 m/s?
O: *50 J | 20 J | 100 J | 10 J
W: $KE = \frac{1}{2} m v^2$. | $KE = 0.5 \times 4 \times 5^2 = 0.5 \times 4 \times 25$. | $= 50$ J.

@lesson phys9-u4-l4-3
C: A motor lifts a 50 kg load through 10 m in 20 s. What is its useful power output? ($g = 10$ m/s²)
O: *250 W | 5000 W | 25 W | 100 W
W: Work done = $mgh = 50 \times 10 \times 10 = 5000$ J. | Power = work ÷ time. | $P = 5000 / 20 = 250$ W.
