C: Which cost does NOT change with the level of output in the short run?
O: *Fixed cost, such as rent | Variable cost, such as raw materials | Total variable cost | Marginal cost
W: Fixed costs must be paid whatever the output, even at zero output. | Rent and insurance are examples. | Raw materials and other variable costs rise as output increases.

C: According to the law of diminishing marginal utility, as a consumer takes more units of a good, ____.
O: *the extra satisfaction from each additional unit falls | total satisfaction always falls | the price must rise | marginal utility keeps increasing
W: Marginal utility is the extra satisfaction from one more unit. | Each additional unit usually gives less satisfaction than the one before. | Total utility may still rise, but more slowly.
