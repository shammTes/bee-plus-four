C: Nuclear fusion is the process in which ____.
O: *light nuclei join to form a heavier nucleus, releasing energy | a heavy nucleus splits into smaller nuclei | an electron is removed from an atom | a nucleus emits only a gamma ray
W: Fusion combines light nuclei such as hydrogen isotopes into helium. | The product has slightly less mass; the lost mass is released as energy (E = mc²). | Splitting a heavy nucleus is fission.

@lesson chem12-u3-l3-6
C: Which radioisotope is used to treat thyroid disorders?
O: *Iodine-131 | Carbon-14 | Uranium-238 | Hydrogen-1
W: The thyroid gland absorbs iodine. | Radioactive iodine-131 collects there and its radiation destroys overactive thyroid tissue. | Carbon-14 is used for dating; U-238 is not used in medicine.

@lesson chem12-u3-l3-7
C: Why is radioactive waste from nuclear power stations dangerous for a long time?
O: *Some of its isotopes have very long half-lives | It is not radioactive | It decays completely in a few seconds | It is only harmful when frozen
W: Spent fuel contains isotopes with half-lives of thousands of years. | They keep emitting ionising radiation that can damage cells and cause cancer. | So the waste must be stored safely for very long periods.
