R: Key formulas of coordinate geometry
B: **Distance** between $A(x_1, y_1)$ and $B(x_2, y_2)$: $AB = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}$.
B: **Midpoint** of $AB$: $M = \left(\frac{x_1 + x_2}{2}, \frac{y_1 + y_2}{2}\right)$.
B: **Slope** of $AB$: $m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}$ ($x_1 \ne x_2$). Parallel lines have equal slopes; perpendicular lines have slopes whose product is $-1$.
B: Example: for $A(1, 2)$ and $B(4, 6)$, $AB = \sqrt{3^2 + 4^2} = 5$, the midpoint is $(2.5, 4)$ and the slope is $\frac{4}{3}$.
