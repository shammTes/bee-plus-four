C: Which is the most important requirement of a good animal house?
O: *Good ventilation with protection from rain, wind and extreme heat | Complete darkness at all times | A floor that stays wet | No access for cleaning
W: Animals need fresh air to prevent respiratory disease and heat stress. | The house must protect them from rain, wind, sun and predators. | It should also be dry and easy to clean, so the other options are wrong.

@lesson agri12-u2-l2-5
C: Which is a major challenge facing forestry and wildlife in Eritrea?
O: *Deforestation and loss of habitat caused by fuelwood cutting, overgrazing and land clearing | Too much rainfall everywhere | An excess of forest cover | Lack of any wild animals
W: Fuelwood demand, grazing and clearing for farms remove trees. | Loss of forest destroys wildlife habitats. | Eritrea has low forest cover and limited rainfall, so the other options are false.
