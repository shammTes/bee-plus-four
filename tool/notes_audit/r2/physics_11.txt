# Physics 11 round 2

@lesson phys11-u3-l3-2
C: Sound waves are ____.
O: *longitudinal mechanical waves that need a medium | transverse electromagnetic waves | waves that travel fastest in a vacuum | waves that do not carry energy
W: Sound is produced by vibrations that make particles of the medium move back and forth. | The particles vibrate parallel to the direction of travel: longitudinal waves. | Sound needs a medium and cannot travel through a vacuum.

@lesson phys11-u3-l3-3
C: An echo is heard 2 s after a shout towards a cliff. If sound travels at 340 m/s, how far away is the cliff?
O: *340 m | 680 m | 170 m | 1360 m
W: The sound travels to the cliff and back: total distance = $v t = 340 \times 2 = 680$ m. | The cliff distance is half of this. | $680 / 2 = 340$ m.

@lesson phys11-u3-l3-4
C: If the intensity of a sound increases by a factor of 10, its intensity level increases by ____.
O: *10 dB | 1 dB | 100 dB | 20 dB
W: Intensity level $\beta = 10 \log(I/I_0)$. | Multiplying $I$ by 10 adds $10 \log 10 = 10$ dB. | So the level rises by 10 dB.

@lesson phys11-u3-l3-5
C: Two instruments play the same note at the same loudness, but they sound different. This difference is called ____.
O: *quality (timbre) | pitch | amplitude | frequency
W: Same note means the same fundamental frequency, so the pitch is the same. | Same loudness means similar amplitude. | The difference comes from the mix of overtones – the quality or timbre.

@lesson phys11-u3-l3-6
C: Which describes noise rather than a musical sound?
O: *An irregular mixture of many frequencies with no regular pattern | A regular vibration with a definite pitch | A sound with a clear fundamental frequency and overtones | A sound produced by a tuning fork
W: Musical sounds come from regular, periodic vibrations. | Noise has irregular vibrations with a random mixture of frequencies. | So noise has no definite pitch.

@lesson phys11-u3-l3-7
C: A radio wave has a frequency of 100 MHz. What is its wavelength? ($c = 3\times10^8$ m/s)
O: *3 m | 0.3 m | 30 m | 300 m
W: $c = f\lambda$, so $\lambda = c/f$. | $\lambda = 3\times10^8 / 1\times10^8$. | $= 3$ m.
