## Chapter Content (English)

## Cohesive Devices & Sentence Connectors

**Definition:** **Cohesive Devices and Sentence Connectors** (መተሓሓዚ ቃላትን ሓረጋትን) are transitional words and phrases used to logically and structurally connect clauses, sentences, and paragraphs, establishing clear relationships of contrast, addition, cause-and-effect, result, sequence, and purpose.

Sentence connectors are the logical framework of English discourse. They do not merely show semantic connections; they also dictate strict syntactic and punctuation rules. In Tigrinya, cohesive relationships are often expressed morphologically through prefixes or suffixes attached directly to verbs (e.g., "ስለዝሓመመ" [because he was sick] or "ጻዕሪ እንተገበረ እኳ" [although he made an effort]). In English, these relationships require independent words that are divided into distinct grammatical classes (conjunctions, prepositions, or conjunctive adverbs), each requiring different sentence patterns. This grammatical division represents a major source of errors on national examinations for Tigrinya-speaking students.

Concessive connectors of contrast (መተሓሓዚ ቃላት ኣንጻር ቃና) represent a highly tested category. These are strictly divided into clause-governing concessives (*although, even though, though*) and noun-governing concessives (*despite, in spite of*). Clause-governing concessives must be followed by a subject and a verb ($$\text{although} + \text{Subject} + \text{Verb}$$). Conversely, noun-governing concessives must strictly be followed by a noun phrase or a gerund ($$\text{despite} + \text{Noun Phrase / V-ing}$$). Tigrinya speakers often translate both patterns under a single conceptual umbrella like "ምስ" or "እኳ እንተ-", leading to common errors such as writing $$\times \text{Although his hard work...}$$ or $$\times \text{Despite he worked hard...}$$ on exams.

Connectors of purpose (መተሓሓዚ ቃላት ዕላማ) also follow strict syntactic guidelines. Infinitive-based purpose markers (*in order to, so as to*) must strictly be followed by a bare verb ($$\text{in order to} + \text{V}_{\text{base}}$$). On the other hand, clause-based purpose markers (*so that, in order that*) require a subject and a modal verb ($$\text{so that} + \text{Subject} + \text{can/could/may/might} + \text{V}_{\text{base}}$$). Because Tigrinya translates both patterns using the prefix "ክ-" or the particle "ምእንቲ" (e.g., "ምእንቲ ክሓልፍ" for both *in order to pass* and *so that he can pass*), students frequently mix these two distinct structural patterns on examinations.

Double conjunction errors (ናይ ድርብ መስተጻምር ጌጋታት) are another common L1 interference challenge. In Tigrinya, it is natural and grammatically correct to pair a cause marker with a result marker (e.g., "ስለ ዝሓመመ፡ ስለዚ ከይዱ" — literally: *Because he was sick, therefore he went*) or a concession with a contrast (e.g., "ድኻ እኳ እንተኾነ፡ ግን እሙን እዩ" — literally: *Although he is poor, but he is honest*). In English, however, a sentence must have only one subordinating or coordinating conjunction per clause link. Writing $$\times \text{Because... so...}$$ or $$\times \text{Although... but...}$$ is a major syntactic error on national exams, as only a single connector is allowed to join the clauses.

Connectors of addition (*furthermore, moreover, in addition*) and result (*therefore, consequently, as a result, thus*) are conjunctive adverbs. When used to link two independent clauses, they follow strict punctuation guidelines: they must be preceded by a semicolon or period and followed immediately by a comma ($$\text{Clause}_1; \text{therefore}, \text{Clause}_2$$). Failing to apply this punctuation is tested heavily as a sentence boundary and run-on error on exams. Students must recognize these punctuation marks as structural requirements rather than optional choices.

To succeed on exam questions, students should apply the **Syntax-Semantic Diagnostic Strategy**. When faced with a sentence completion item, first read the entire sentence to identify the logical relationship between the clauses (e.g., contrast, addition, or result). Second, examine the structure immediately following the blank. If it is a noun phrase, eliminate clause-based options like *although* or *because*. If it is a verb in the base form, look for *in order to* or *so as to*. Third, verify that no double conjunctions are present in the sentence. Applying this systematic probe ensures that students avoid L1 translation traps and select the correct connector.

| Connector Category | Grammatical Pattern / Formula | Example | Tigrinya Concept Equivalent |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Clause Contrast** | $$\text{although / even though} + \text{S} + \text{V}$$ | *Although he studied, he failed.* | እኳ እንተ-... (Concessive clause) |
| **Noun Contrast** | $$\text{despite / in spite of} + \text{Noun/V-ing}$$ | *Despite studying, he failed.* | ምስቲ... (Concessive preposition) |
| **Infinitive Purpose** | $$\text{in order to / so as to} + \text{V}_{\text{base}}$$ | *He studied in order to pass.* | ምእንቲ ክ-... (Infinitive purpose) |
| **Clause Purpose** | $$\text{so that} + \text{Subject} + \text{modal} + \text{V}$$ | *He studied so that he could pass.* | ምእንቲ ክ-... (Clausal purpose) |
| **Conjunctive Adverb** | $$\text{Clause}_1; \text{therefore / moreover}, \text{Clause}_2$$ | *He studied; therefore, he passed.* | ስለዚ / ብተወሳኺ (With strict punctuation) |

> **💡 Did You Know?**: The word *cohesion* comes from the Latin word *cohaerere*, which literally means "to stick together"! Just like glue holds physical objects together, cohesive devices act as the structural glue of English, holding sentences and clauses together to ensure your writing does not fall apart into disconnected fragments!

> **🧠 Pro-Tip**: To easily solve **Although vs. Despite** questions under intense exam time pressure, apply the **Verb-Scan Test**! Look at the words immediately following the blank up to the comma: 
- If you see a conjugated verb (like *is, was, has, worked, went*), always choose **although** or **even though** ($$	ext{although} + 	ext{Subject} + 	ext{Verb}$$).
- If there is no verb (or only a gerund ending in *-ing*), always choose **despite** or **in spite of** ($$	ext{despite} + 	ext{Noun/Gerund}$$). This quick scan will help you find the correct option instantly! 

### 📝 Topic Challenge (Moderate to Hard)

1. Choose the grammatically correct option: "________ having outstanding matriculation results, Dawit did not secure the national scholarship."
A. Although
B. Despite
C. Because
D. Even though
*Answer: B. The blank is followed by a gerund phrase ("having outstanding matriculation results") and introduces contrast. This requires the noun-governing concessive preposition "Despite". Option A and D require a full clause ($$\text{Subject} + \text{Verb}$$).*

2. Fill in the blank: "The chemistry teacher explained the laboratory experiment slowly ________ the students could follow the steps."
A. in order to
B. so that
C. so as to
D. because of
*Answer: B. The blank is followed by a full clause containing a modal verb ("the students could follow the steps"), which indicates a purpose relationship. This requires the clause-based purpose connector "so that" ($$\text{so that} + \text{S} + \text{modal} + \text{V}$$).*

3. Choose the correct option: "The school compound was heavily flooded; ________, all physical education classes were canceled."
A. because of
B. consequently
C. furthermore
D. although
*Answer: B. The blank is preceded by a semicolon and followed by a comma, indicating a conjunctive adverb is required. The logical relationship is cause-and-effect, making "consequently" (as a result) correct. "Furthermore" indicates addition.*

4. Complete the sentence: "Although she spent her entire weekend preparing for the biology test, ________ she failed to achieve a passing mark."
A. but
B. yet
C. [No connector needed]
D. so
*Answer: C. Because "Although" is already present at the beginning of the sentence to connect the clauses, adding another coordinating conjunction like "but" (Option A) or "so" (Option D) is a grammatical double-conjunction error. No extra connector is needed in the main clause.*

5. Identify the error: "Because of he was late for the final registration, the clerk refused to accept his application form."
*Answer: The error is "Because of". "Because of" is a preposition and must be followed by a noun phrase. Here, it is followed by a full clause ("he was late"), which strictly requires the subordinating conjunction "Because" ($$\text{Because} + \text{S} + \text{V}$$). The corrected sentence should read: "Because he was late...".*

## Review Exercises (30)

### 1. ________ the heavy storm and road blockages, the administrative staff arrived at the school on time.

*Type:* MC  |  *Topic:* Contrast Connectors

- Although
- Despite
- Even though
- In spite

**Answer:** Despite

**Explanation:**

**Step 1: Analyze the grammatical structure**
The blank is followed by a noun phrase 'the heavy storm and road blockages' (no verb present) and introduces contrast. This requires a noun-governing concessive preposition: $$\text{despite / in spite of} + \text{Noun Phrase}$$.

**Step 2: Identify the matriculation trap**
Students often choose 'Although' (Option A) or 'Even though' (Option C) because they translate 'እኳ እንተ-... ምስ' similarly. Option D 'In spite' is incorrect because it lacks 'of'. Option B is correct.

**Step 3: Tigrinya Explanation (ትርጉም)**
እቲ ባዶ ቦታ ዝስዕቦ ሓረግ 'the heavy storm...' ስም (noun phrase) እምበር ግሲ (verb) የብሉን። ስለዚ ምስ ስም ዝኸይድ ናይ ኣንጻር መተሓሓዚ ቃል 'Despite' (Option B - ምስቲ ከቢድ ዝናብ) ጥራይ እዩ። 'Although' እንተይልና ግን ብግድን ምሉእ ሓሳብ ($$Subject + Verb$$) ክስዕቦ ነይርዎ።


### 2. The final chemistry revision class was canceled ________ the sudden illness of the professor.

*Type:* MC  |  *Topic:* Reason Connectors

- because
- because of
- since
- as

**Answer:** because of

**Explanation:**

**Step 1: Analyze the grammatical structure**
The blank is followed by a noun phrase 'the sudden illness of the professor', indicating a cause-and-effect relationship. This requires a prepositional cause marker: $$\text{because of} + \text{Noun Phrase}$$.

**Step 2: Identify the matriculation trap**
Students often choose 'because' (Option A) or 'since' (Option C) because they translate both as 'ስለ...'. However, 'because' requires a full clause ($$\text{Subject} + \text{Verb}$$), while 'because of' (Option B) is correct for noun phrases.

**Step 3: Tigrinya Explanation (ትርጉም)**
እቲ ባዶ ቦታ ዝስዕቦ ሓረግ ስም (noun phrase) ስለ ዝኾነ፡ ምስኡ ዝኸይድ 'because of' (Option B - ብሰንኪ) እዩ። 'Because' እንተይልና ግን ምሉእ ሓሳብ ($$Subject + Verb$$) ክስዕቦ ነይርዎ (e.g., because the professor was ill)።


### 3. Although the laboratory equipment was extremely expensive, ________ we decided to purchase it for our research.

*Type:* MC  |  *Topic:* Double Conjunction Trap

- but
- yet
- and
- [No connector]

**Answer:** [No connector]

**Explanation:**

**Step 1: Analyze the grammatical structure**
'Although' is a subordinating conjunction at the beginning of the sentence to connect the clauses. In English, only one conjunction can connect two clauses; adding a second is a double-conjunction error.

**Step 2: Identify the matriculation trap**
Students often choose 'but' (Option A) because in Tigrinya, it is natural to pair concession with contrast (e.g., 'ድኻ እኳ እንተኾነ፡ ግን...'). Option D is correct because no extra connector is allowed.

**Step 3: Tigrinya Explanation (ትርጉም)**
ኣብ መጀመርታ እቲ ምሉእ ሓሳብ 'Although' (እኳ እንተ-) ዝብል መተሓሓዚ ስለ ዘሎ፡ ኣብቲ ካልኣይ ሓረግ 'but' (ግን) ወይ 'yet' ምጥቃም ዓቢ ናይ ድርብ መስተጻምር ጌጋ (double-conjunction error) ይፈጥር። ስለዚ ቅኑዕ መልሲ Option D ([No connector] - ዋላ ሓደ ቃል ኣየድልይን) እዩ።


### 4. She studied hard until midnight ________ she could pass the national biology examination.

*Type:* MC  |  *Topic:* Purpose Connectors

- in order to
- so that
- so as to
- because of

**Answer:** so that

**Explanation:**

**Step 1: Analyze the grammatical structure**
The blank is followed by a full clause containing a modal verb ('she could pass...'). This requires a clause-based purpose connector: $$\text{so that} + \text{Subject} + \text{modal} + \text{Verb}$$.

**Step 2: Identify the matriculation trap**
Students often choose 'in order to' (Option A) or 'so as to' (Option C) because they translate both as 'ምእንቲ'. However, those infinitive-based connectors require a bare verb ($$\text{in order to} + \text{V}_{\text{base}}$$), making Option B correct.

**Step 3: Tigrinya Explanation (ትርጉም)**
እቲ ባዶ ቦታ ዝስዕቦ ሓረግ ምሉእ ሓሳብ ኮይኑ 'could' (modal) ዝብል ቃል ስለ ዘለዎ፡ ምስኡ ዝኸይድ ናይ ዕላማ መተሓሓዚ ቃል 'so that' (Option B - ምእንቲ ክትሓልፍ) እዩ። 'in order to' እንተይልና ግን ብግድን 'pass' (base verb) ጥራይ ክስዕቦ ነይርዎ (e.g., in order to pass)።


### 5. ________ being extremely tired after the long journey, she completed her chemistry homework.

*Type:* MC  |  *Topic:* Contrast Connectors

- Although
- In spite of
- Even though
- Because

**Answer:** In spite of

**Step 2: Identify the matriculation trap**

ድሕሪ እቲ ባዶ ቦታ 'being' (gerund - V-ing) ስለ ዘሎ፡ ምስኡ ዝኸይድ ናይ ኣንጻር መተሓሓዚ ቃል 'In spite of' (Option B - ብሰንኪ ድኻም) እዩ። 'Although' ወይ 'Even though' ክኸውን ኣይኽእልን ምኽንያቱ ድሕሪኦም ምሉእ ሓሳብ ($$Subject + Verb$$) ስለ ዝደልዩ።


### 6. He studied the grammar modules diligently; ________, he achieved a top score on the ESSLCE.

*Type:* MC  |  *Topic:* Result Connectors

- consequently
- because
- furthermore
- although

**Answer:** consequently

**Explanation:**

**Step 1: Analyze the grammatical structure**

**Step 2: Identify the matriculation trap**

### 7. We will not be able to finish the project on time ________ everyone works much harder.

*Type:* MC  |  *Topic:* Conditional Connectors

- if
- unless
- provided that
- as long as

**Answer:** unless

**Explanation:**

**Step 1: Analyze the grammatical structure**
The sentence expresses a negative condition: we won't finish *unless* (if not) everyone works harder. This requires the negative conditional conjunction 'unless' ($$\text{unless} = \text{if} + \text{not}$$).

**Step 2: Identify the matriculation trap**
Students often choose 'if' (Option A) because they translate 'እንድሕር' directly. However, using 'if' would mean 'if everyone works harder, we won't finish', which is logically incorrect. Option B 'unless' is correct.

**Step 3: Tigrinya Explanation (ትርጉም)**
እቲ ትርጉም 'ነፍሲ ወከፍ ሰብ እንተዘይጽዒሩ (if not)፡ ነቲ ፕሮጀክት ኣብ እዋኑ ክንውድኦ ኣይንኽእልን' ዝብል እዩ። 'እንተዘይኮይኑ' ንምግላጽ ድማ 'unless' (Option B) ክንጥቀም ኣለና። 'if' ምጥቃም ትርጉም የባላሽዎ።

### 10. Aster prefers studying physics, ________ her younger sister loves biology.

*Type:* MC  |  *Topic:* Contrast Connectors

- whereas
- furthermore
- despite
- in order to

**Answer:** whereas

**Explanation:**

**Step 1: Analyze the grammatical structure**
The sentence compares two differing preferences directly (contrast), requiring a subordinating conjunction of contrast: $$\text{Clause}_1 + \text{whereas} + \text{Clause}_2$$.

**Step 2: Identify the matriculation trap**

The blank is followed by a gerund 'studying' (no subject present) and introduces addition. This requires a prepositional addition marker: $$\text{In addition to} + \text{Noun/V-ing}$$.

**Step 2: Identify the matriculation trap**
Students often choose 'Furthermore' (Option B) or 'Moreover' (Option C) because they see addition. However, those are conjunctive adverbs and must be followed by a full clause ($$\text{Subject} + \text{Verb}$$). Option A is correct.

**Step 3: Tigrinya Explanation (ትርጉም)**
ድሕሪ እቲ ባዶ ቦታ 'studying' (gerund - V-ing) ስለ ዘሎ፡ ምስኡ ዝኸይድ ናይ ወሰኽ መተሓሓዚ ቃል 'In addition to' (Option A - ብተወሳኺ ምስራሕ) እዩ። 'Furthermore' ወይ 'Moreover' ክኸውን ኣይኽእልን ምኽንያቱ ድሕሪኦም ምሉእ ሓሳብ ($$Subject + Verb$$) ስለ ዝደልዩ።


### 26. Complete the sentence: 'He woke up early ________ he wouldn't miss the bus.'

*Type:* WORKOUT  |  *Topic:* Purpose Connectors

- in order to
- so that
- so as to
- because of

**Answer:** so that

**Explanation:**

**Step 1: Analyze the grammatical structure**
The blank is followed by a full clause containing a modal verb ('he wouldn't miss...'). This requires a clause-based purpose connector: $$\text{so that} + \text{Subject} + \text{modal} + \text{Verb}$$.

**Step 2: Identify the matriculation trap**
Students often choose 'in order to' (Option A) or 'so as to' (Option C) because they translate both as 'ምእንቲ'. However, those infinitive-based connectors require a bare verb ($$\text{in order to} + \text{V}_{\text{base}}$$), making Option B correct.

**Step 3: Tigrinya Explanation (ትርጉም)**
እቲ ባዶ ቦታ ዝስዕቦ ሓረግ ምሉእ ሓሳብ ኮይኑ 'wouldn't' (modal) ዝብል ቃል ስለ ዘለዎ፡ ምስኡ ዝኸይድ ናይ ዕላማ መተሓሓዚ ቃል 'so that' (Option B - ምእንቲ ከይረስት) እዩ። 'in order to' እንተይልና ግን ብግድን 'miss' (base verb) ጥራይ ክስዕቦ ነይርዎ።


### 27. Complete the sentence with the correct contrast connector: '________ she worked hard, she failed to pass the test.'

*Type:* WORKOUT  |  *Topic:* Contrast Connectors

- Although
- Despite
- Because
- Unless

**Answer:** Although

**Explanation:**

**Step 1: Analyze the grammatical structure**
The blank is followed by a full clause 'she worked hard' (Subject + Verb) and introduces contrast. This requires a clause-governing concessive conjunction: $$\text{Although} + \text{Subject} + \text{Verb}$$.

**Step 2: Identify the matriculation trap**
Students often choose 'Despite' (Option B) because they translate 'ምስ' directly. However, 'Despite' requires a noun phrase, making 'Although' (Option A) correct.

**Step 3: Tigrinya Explanation (ትርጉም)**
ድሕሪ እቲ ባዶ ቦታ ምሉእ ሓሳብ ($$Subject + Verb$$ - 'she worked...') ስለ ዘሎ፡ ምስኡ ዝኸይድ ናይ ኣንጻር መተሓሓዚ ቃል 'Although' (Option A - እኳ እንተጸዓረት) እዩ። 'Despite' ክኸውን ኣይኽእልን ምኽንያቱ ድሕሪኡ ስም (noun phrase) ጥራይ ስለ ዝወስድ።


### 28. Complete the sentence with the correct contrast connector: '________ the fact that she worked hard, she failed to pass the test.'

*Type:* WORKOUT  |  *Topic:* Contrast Connectors

- Although
- Despite
- Because
- Unless

**Answer:** Despite

**Explanation:**

**Step 1: Analyze the grammatical structure**
The blank is followed by the phrase 'the fact that' (which acts as a noun phrase block, even though it is followed by a clause). This requires a noun-governing concessive preposition: $$\text{despite} + \text{the fact that} + \text{Clause}$$.

**Step 2: Identify the matriculation trap**
Students often choose 'Although' (Option A) because they see 'she worked hard' at the end. However, because 'the fact that' is inserted, the prepositional 'Despite' (Option B) is correct.

**Step 3: Tigrinya Explanation (ትርጉም)**
ድሕሪ እቲ ባዶ ቦታ 'the fact that...' ዝብል ስማዊ ሓረግ ስለ ዘሎ፡ ምስኡ ዝኸይድ 'Despite' (Option B - ምስቲ ሓቂ) እዩ። 'Although' ክኸውን ኣይኽእልን ምኽንያቱ ድሕሪኡ በቀጥታ ምሉእ ሓሳብ ስለ ዘይመጸ።


### 29. Complete the sentence: 'Aster prefers studying physics; her sister, ________, loves biology.'

*Type:* WORKOUT  |  *Topic:* Contrast Connectors

- whereas
- on the other hand
- despite
- in order to

**Answer:** on the other hand

**Explanation:**

**Step 1: Analyze the grammatical structure**
The blank is placed parenthetically inside the second clause (surrounded by commas). This position requires a transitional phrase of contrast: $$\text{Subject, on the other hand, Verb} \dots$$.

**Step 2: Identify the matriculation trap**
Students often choose 'whereas' (Option A), but 'whereas' is a subordinating conjunction and cannot be placed parenthetically after the subject. Option B 'on the other hand' is correct.

**Step 3: Tigrinya Explanation (ትርጉም)**
እቲ ባዶ ቦታ ኣብ ማእከል እቲ ካልኣይ ሓረግ ብኮማታት ተኸቢቡ ስለ ዘሎ፡ ብግድን parenthetical transitional phrase ክኸውን ኣለዎ። ስለዚ ምስዚ ኣገባብ ዝኸይድ ናይ ኣንጻር መተሓሓዚ ቃል 'on the other hand' (Option B - በቲ ካልእ ወገን) እዩ።


### 30. Identify the option that corrects the double conjunction error: 'Because he was late, so he missed the bus.'

*Type:* WORKOUT  |  *Topic:* Double Conjunction Trap

- Because he was late, he missed the bus.
- Because he was late, so he missed the bus.
- Because he was late, but he missed the bus.
- He was late, so because he missed the bus.

**Answer:** Because he was late, he missed the bus.

**Explanation:**

**Step 1: Analyze the grammatical structure**
In English, a sentence must have only one conjunction per clause link. Pairing 'Because' with 'so' is a grammatical double-conjunction error.

**Step 2: Identify the matriculation trap**
Students often choose 'so' (original sentence) because in Tigrinya, it is natural to pair cause with result. Option A correctly removes 'so', leaving only 'Because' to connect the clauses.

**Step 3: Tigrinya Explanation (ትርጉም)**
ኣብ መጀመርታ እቲ ምሉእ ሓሳብ 'Because' (ምኽንያቱ) ዝብል መተሓሓዚ ስለ ዘሎ፡ ኣብቲ ካልኣይ ሓረግ 'so' (ስለዚ) ምጥቃም ዓቢ ናይ ድርብ መስተጻምር ጌጋ ይፈጥር። ስለዚ ቅኑዕ መልሲ 'Because he was late, he missed the bus' (Option A - ስለ ዝደንገየ፡ ነታ ባስ ስሒትዋ) እዩ።
